# Photo booth gallery

Nine illustrative stock portraits, not photographs of Sundown Press customers or a record of actual booth sessions. Optimised 420px AVIF images are included in `public/images/photobooth/` and served locally. The Pexels image CDN supplied the originals. Update the IDs in `src/photobooth.js` and corresponding local assets to change the collection.

Source: [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/).

The gallery recreates the draggable, repeating image-plane interaction of the selected [Originkit Infinity Canvas reference](https://www.originkit.dev/components/infinitegallery?preset=base). It is a native DOM implementation adapted to the site's Vite entry, without the original Framer component source.
